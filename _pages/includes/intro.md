I am a Postdoctoral Researcher in the [Gangarosa Department of Environmental Health](https://sph.emory.edu/departments/eh/) at [Emory University](https://www.emory.edu/)'s Rollins School of Public Health. As part of a [Health Effects Institute (HEI)](https://www.healtheffects.org/)-funded project, I work on improving satellite-based air pollution exposure assessment and understanding how unconventional oil and gas development affects human health.

My research brings together satellite remote sensing, geospatial AI, and spatiotemporal data integration, with a focus on satellite validation and uncertainty characterization to produce reliable exposure estimates for environmental health research. I received my Ph.D. in Earth Systems and Geoinformation Science from George Mason University in December 2025.

# 🔬 Research Interests

- Remote Sensing Applications on Air Quality Monitoring
- Geographic Information Systems (GIS)
- Spatial Cloud Computing
- Geospatial AI and Machine Learning
- Uncertainty Quantification
- Spatial Data Fusion
