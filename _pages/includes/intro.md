I am a Postdoctoral Researcher in the [Gangarosa Department of Environmental Health](https://sph.emory.edu/departments/eh/) at [Emory University](https://www.emory.edu/)'s Rollins School of Public Health. As part of a [Health Effects Institute (HEI)](https://www.healtheffects.org/) study on the health impacts of unconventional oil and gas development, I focus on uncertainty quantification for satellite-based air pollution exposure assessment: validating satellite products against ground observations and characterizing how retrieval conditions, viewing geometry, clouds, and spatiotemporal sampling affect the reliability of exposure estimates.

Uncertainty quantification is the thread that runs through my research. I develop methods that show not only what satellite and AI/ML models predict, but how far those predictions can be trusted, drawing on satellite remote sensing, geospatial AI, and spatiotemporal data integration. I received my Ph.D. in Earth Systems and Geoinformation Science from George Mason University in December 2025. My work has been cited <strong><span id='total_cit'>175</span>+</strong> times on [Google Scholar](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=vDCNltwAAAAJ).
