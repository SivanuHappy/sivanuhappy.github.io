I am a Postdoctoral Researcher in the [Gangarosa Department of Environmental Health](https://sph.emory.edu/departments/eh/) at [Emory University](https://www.emory.edu/). My current work is part of a [Health Effects Institute (HEI)](https://www.healtheffects.org/)-funded project focused on improving satellite-based air pollution exposure assessment and understanding the health impacts of unconventional oil and gas development.

My research focuses on satellite remote sensing, satellite validation, uncertainty characterization, geospatial AI, and spatiotemporal data integration. I completed my Ph.D. in Earth Systems and Geoinformation Science at George Mason University in December 2025.

# 🔬 Research Interests

- Remote Sensing Applications on Air Quality Monitoring
- Geographic Information Systems (GIS)
- Spatial Cloud Computing
- Geospatial AI and Machine Learning
- Uncertainty Quantification
- Spatial Data Fusion
